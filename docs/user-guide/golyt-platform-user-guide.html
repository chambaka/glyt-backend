A

Platform overview

What GoLyt does, who uses each part of it, and how a bag moves from Kilimanjaro to a Zanzibar pickup desk.

Part A · Overview

A1. What GoLyt is

Custody, not GPSKIA → ZNZPilot corridor

GoLyt lets travellers fly light between Kilimanjaro International Airport (KIA) and Zanzibar (ZNZ). Trained officers take custody of the luggage at KIA, seal it, hand it to the airline by manifest, receive and store it in Zanzibar, and release it to the verified owner at a pickup desk.

Tracking is based on custody events recorded by officers (received, sealed, handed over, received, stored, ready, collected) — not live GPS positions.

A2. The four surfaces

SurfaceWho uses itWhat it is for
Public website Travellers Learn about the service, see prices, get a quote, create an account, view bookings, track a bag, contact support.
Officer phone app KIA_OFFICER, ZNZ_OFFICER Register bags, take photos, apply seals, scan QR labels, build and receive manifests, shelve and release bags.
Ops console (web) ADMIN, READONLY Monitor the corridor, work exceptions and support, review bookings and payments, manage facilities, catalog, users, reports and audits.
Backend API All of the above (behind the scenes) Single source of truth for bags, bookings, payments, manifests, notifications and audit history.
This guide documents the public website (Part B), the ops console (Part C), and the officer phone app (Part E) in detail. Part A’s end-to-end flow shows how the three surfaces fit together.

B2. How it works

4 steps

A short explanation of the four steps from booking to collection. It stresses that GoLyt provides custody, not GPS tracking.

  1. Book — choose a service online, or register at KIA intake with an officer.
  2. Secure — a QR label, photos and seals create a sealed custody record.
  3. Transfer — manifest handover moves bags safely toward Zanzibar storage.
  4. Collect — you receive a ready notice, then a verified release at the pickup desk.

Ops console — administrator guide

A walkthrough of every menu available after an ADMIN signs in to the GoLyt operations console, from corridor custody to staff administration.

Part C · Getting started

Before you start

  • Open the ops console in a browser (local default: http://localhost:5180).
  • Only ADMIN and READONLY accounts use this web console. KIA / Zanzibar officers use the phone app.
  • Every staff account needs a valid Tanzania phone number for SMS password delivery.
  • Access tokens last about 15 minutes; refresh tokens about 8 hours. If refresh fails, you are signed out automatically.
  • The Users menu is visible only to ADMIN. There is no 2FA on this console.

C1. Sign in

AuthADMIN / READONLYSMS reset

Use your staff email and password, then choose Enter console.

  • Forgot password sends a short SMS code to the phone on your account (not an email link).
  • Enter the SMS code and a new password that meets the staff rules, then sign in again.
  • If an account requires a password change, you must complete it before the menus unlock.
  • Officer accounts (KIA / ZNZ) are blocked here and directed to the phone app.
Password rules: at least 10 characters, with uppercase, lowercase, a number, and a special character from !@#$%^&*. It must not include your name or email local-part and must not be a common password.

E6. KIA receive and seal

ReceivePhotosSeal

Receive at KIA

  1. Confirm weight (kg) and condition (Good / Minor existing damage / Damaged — damage note required if damaged).
  2. Tick Customer confirms personal effects and no prohibited items.
  3. Capture full bag and tag side photos.
  4. Tap Confirm receipt.

Security seal

  1. Enter the seal number; set condition; capture the seal photo.
  2. Bags over 20 kg show an overweight warning — tick Accept overweight and open a supervisor exception if you proceed.
  3. Tap Confirm seal. The bag is then eligible for a flight manifest.
After sealing, print the QR label and share the tracking message with the traveller (WhatsApp or email) from Overview when those actions are available.

D

Daily operations tips

Practical routines for ops leads: keep the SLA queue clean, chase unpaid bookings, and close the day with confidence.

Part D · Daily operations

D1. Start-of-shift routine

  1. Dashboard — scan the cards and alerts. Anything red (open exceptions, seal issues, overdue collections) goes first.
  2. Workload — check Overdue SLA, Open support, Pending payments and Ready for collection counts.
  3. Manifests — confirm today’s flights have manifests and the status matches what officers report.
  4. Support — assign any unowned OPEN tickets.
  5. Payments — filter Pending to see money that has not settled (see D3).

D2. Working the SLA queue

WorkloadExceptions

The SLA queue on Workload is your priority list. Each row is an exception with a due time.

PriorityHow to treat it
CRITICALAct immediately. Seal mismatch or damaged seals can affect release — contact the responsible officer now.
HIGHHandle before routine work, e.g. a damaged bag that must be documented before collection.
LowerHandle in due-time order.
  1. Sort mentally by overdue first (tinted rows), then by priority, then by due time.
  2. Look at the Assignee. Unassigned items need an owner; use Staff load to find who has capacity.
  3. Open the bag in Bags to read status, seal and location before you decide.
  4. Fix the real issue (re-seal, re-check, contact the airline or customer), then use Resolve on the exception with a clear note — what happened and what was done.
  5. Refresh Workload to confirm the item has left the queue and the overdue count dropped.
Good resolution notes save time later: the audit trail and the Exceptions report both show them.
The exact SLA durations per exception type are set by the platform configuration. Follow the due time shown in the queue rather than assuming a fixed number of hours.
Part D · Daily operations

D3. Unpaid and pending bookings

PaymentsBookingsSupport

A booking can exist before money has settled. Chasing these early prevents surprises at collection time.

Find them

  1. Open Payments and set Status to Pending, then Failed. Note the booking number on each row.
  2. Open Bookings and search for those booking numbers. Check the KIA arrival and Zanzibar travel dates — the closer the date, the higher the priority.
  3. On Workload, the Pending payments card gives the headline count to track through the day.

Act on them

SituationSuggested action
Pending, travel date is today or tomorrowContact the customer by phone first. Confirm they intend to pay and how (mobile money, card or at the desk).
Failed paymentAsk the customer to retry or use another method; create or update a support note so the next shift knows.
Pending for a long time, no responseEscalate to the ops lead. Customer may cancel the booking from their account.
Customer says they paid but status is PendingAsk for the provider reference, compare with the provider statement via Export reconcile CSV, then escalate to finance.
Paid in error or booking cancelled after paymentADMIN: use Refund on the Succeeded payment and enter a refund reason.
Do not release a bag on the strength of a verbal “I paid”. Confirm the payment status in the console (or the receipt number) first.
Always record what you did — a support ticket note or exception note — so that the next officer does not repeat the call.

D4. End-of-day checklist

  • Workload: Overdue SLA is zero, or every remaining item has an owner and a note.
  • Support: no unassigned OPEN tickets.
  • Payments: pending items for tomorrow’s flights have been chased.
  • Manifests: every flight of the day shows the expected status.
  • Reports: download Daily operations (and Exceptions if any were raised) for the record.
  • Audits: scan for unexpected actions (unusual refunds, user changes).

D5. Quick reference

“Where is bag X?”

Console → Bags → search code / name / phone / passport → open row.

“I can’t see my bag online”

Check the traveller has the right bag code and the private token. If the token is lost, an ADMIN can look up the bag and share its message.

“Customer forgot a password”

Customers: website Account page / support. Staff: Forgot password (SMS) on the console, or an ADMIN reset from Users.

“Price changed?”

Catalog edit → website Services / Pricing update. Remember minor units.
